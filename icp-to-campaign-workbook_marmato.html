01 · Analysis

What this curriculum actually teaches, and why it is ordered this way

The material is not a marketing course with an ICP chapter. It is an operating system: a chain of seven stages where each stage consumes the output of the previous one. Break the chain anywhere and the last stage — the campaign — inherits the damage.

  • Decision rules over descriptions. An ICP is only useful if two people reading the same company reach the same verdict. Everything downstream (scoring, routing, spend) is a consequence of that rule.
  • Evidence is the currency. No source URL and date, no points. This single constraint is what stops the system from becoming opinion with a dashboard.
  • Pain and timing beat size. The 100-point model gives 55 points to complexity, problem fit and active trigger versus 20 to firmographics.
  • Routing is arithmetic, not taste. Tier × trigger × awareness × engagement determines the lane. Campaign type is an output, never a starting preference.
  • Most campaign failures are research failures. Diagnose upstream first: coverage and data quality before subject lines.

Golden rule: never research a person before the account qualifies, and never launch a campaign before the account is scored and routed. Research quality caps campaign quality.

Common failure A

40k purchased contacts emailed with no ICP → 0.2% reply, domain reputation damage. Fix is subtraction: keep 1,800 accounts, suppress the rest.

Common failure B

Tier A ABM underperforming Tier C drip. Root cause is usually committee coverage (1.2 contacts/account), not copy.

Common failure C

Personalization on dirty data. Identity resolution and dedupe must precede any enrichment or send.

03 · Delivery plan

Six-week schedule and capstone

Week 1 · Modules 00–01

Write the ICP one-pager and validate it against 10 real companies.

Week 2 · Modules 02–03

Score 30 accounts; research 10 with full evidence and triggers.

Week 3 · Modules 04–05

Clean and dedupe the 30; map committees on the top 10.

Week 4 · Modules 06–07

Build the data layer; write the routing table as workflow logic.

Week 5 · Modules 08–09

Design one drip, one nurture, one ABM play, one trigger sequence.

Week 6 · Modules 10–11

Automate ingest and routing, build the dashboard, run the first review.

Capstone deliverable — a live mini-GTM system

ICP one-pager · 30 scored accounts · 10 researched with evidence · committees mapped on the top 10 · a written routing table · four campaign plays built · a dashboard and a first monthly review. Not a slide deck.
